# Human Annotation Guidelines

Rate each dimension from 1–5: **1 serious issue**, **2 significant issue**, **3 mixed/uncertain**, **4 good**, **5 excellent**.

- **Grammar:** agreement, postpositions, tense/aspect, spelling, punctuation and sentence structure.
- **Semantic relevance:** relevance to the original prompt; store prompt/context with each response.
- **Fluency:** natural phrasing, coherence, readability and register.
- **Cultural appropriateness:** respectful, context-aware language; avoid stereotypes and assumptions about region, caste, religion, gender, age or community.

Record text, prompt/context, four scores, evidence, pseudonymous annotator ID, and uncertainty. Annotate independently; double-annotate at least 20%; calculate weighted Cohen's kappa (two annotators) or Krippendorff's alpha (multiple annotators); adjudicate disagreements. Include dialects and code-mixed Hindi. Do not treat heuristic labels as gold labels.
